Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

MeetSnap is a free, open-source app by Anshu Raj, built for the #BuildHybridAI 2026 hackathon. It helps salespeople capture business contacts at events, qualify them with AI and write follow-up emails. This page explains what data MeetSnap handles and why.

1. Data we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, through Firebase Authentication (email and password, or Sign in with Google). If you continue as a guest, an anonymous account is created instead.
  • Team details: your team name, your company, what you sell, the event name, and a join code.
  • Leads you capture: the contact details you scan or type (for example name, job title, company, email, phone, website), your notes about the conversation, a small thumbnail of the business card, and the AI-generated qualification, company summary and email draft.
  • Voice notes: turned into text. MeetSnap does not store audio. Speech recognition runs on your device where the platform supports it; otherwise your browser's or phone's speech service is used, under its own privacy terms.
  • Technical data: Firebase App Check tokens that protect the service from abuse, and a local log of AI requests (stored only on your device).

2. How AI processes your data

MeetSnap uses hybrid AI through Firebase AI Logic:

  • On-device (Gemini Nano): when your device supports it, card reading and lead scoring run on your device and that content does not leave it.
  • Cloud (Google Gemini API): company research, follow-up emails, and any task your device can't run locally are sent to Google's Gemini API. Google processes this content under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. On the free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, so don't enter information you are not allowed to share.

AI output can be wrong. Company summaries come from the model's general knowledge and are labelled for you to verify.

3. Where data is stored

Your account, team and leads are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Authentication). A copy is cached on your device so the app works offline; it syncs when you reconnect.

4. Who can see your data

  • Leads are visible to members of your team (people who joined with your team's code).
  • We do not sell your data and MeetSnap shows no ads.
  • Data is shared only with the service providers that run MeetSnap: Google (Firebase and the Gemini API).

5. Contacts you capture

When you store someone else's business contact details, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to do so (for example, they gave you their card to be contacted) and for following the rules that apply to your sales outreach.

6. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Firebase App Check limits the AI service to the genuine app, and Firestore security rules let you read and write only your own profile and your own team's leads.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept until it is deleted. Signing out keeps your leads in your account. To ask for your account and data to be deleted, open an issue at github.com/anshuraj669/MeetSnap/issues asking us to contact you. Please don't post personal details in the public issue.

8. Children

MeetSnap is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 16.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
